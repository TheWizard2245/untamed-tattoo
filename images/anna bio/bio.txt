My name is Anna and I am a piercing apprentice here at Untamed. I joined the Untamed family after the world started to open again in 2020 as a receptionist. It wasn't long after "mastering" the front desk that I became infatuated with piercing and the infinite possibilities our anatomy presents. I have always been a lover of body art for the unique self expression & confidence boost it provides, so it was without hesitation I jumped at the opportunity to train hands on under the multi talented Mark Brown and his piercing rock star Lindsey Smith.
My training consists heavily of cross contamination prevention, anatomy education, precision techniques, piercing aftercare & implant grade jewelry standards. This is a constantly evolving field so we are always striving to improve and exceed industry standards while also making support easily accessible throughout our clients piercing / healing journey. I am certified in blood borne pathogens, first aide and CPR.
As a mother myself I love working with kids to help them through their fear and foster self love with bold, brave & beautiful jewelry choices. Nervous clients are welcome as even I, once upon a time, have been afraid of needles! I look forward to fine tuning my craft here at untamed and expanding my portfolio. Hopefully one day you'll be in it, too!
