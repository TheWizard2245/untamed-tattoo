My name is Lindsey and Ive been a body piercer at Untamed for almost a decade . I have been with my amazing husband for over 10 years and we have a beautiful son and two loving pups. I’m originally from New Hampshire, but grew up in Rhode Island most of my life. Some interests of mine outside of my line of work include reading, running, singing, and cooking. I was a huge theater kid growing up so I always love going to shows whenever I can. Becoming a piercer has been an amazing opportunity for me. Ive made it my mission to put client safety and comfort as a priority. As a professional piercer, I am always up to date on our certifications, such as blood-Bourne pathogens, CPR/First aid, and “Stop the bleed” courses. I make sure our techniques and jewelry that we use for our services comply with current industry standards, as they are constantly evolving. My absolute favorite piercings to do are paired lips piercings and ear curations. I love when clients have unique anatomy because it gives me freedom to create something special for them when they feel insecure about parts of their body/the possibilities of not achieving their piercing goals. It’s important to make people feel good about themselves. It’s always an honor to make a clients exterior decoration dreams come true. Come on down to Untamed!
