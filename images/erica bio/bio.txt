My name is Erika Brown, and I became part of the Untamed family in 2021. I work behind the scenes taking care of the administrative side of the business. I also work closely with our piercers Lindsey and Anna on our monthly sales and what’s trending for piercing and jewelry.

I have worked in administration roles for most of my life. My grandfather owned his own business, and when I turned 13 he gave me a job. I would answer the phone, file documents, open the mail, small jobs like that. He was a great man, and he gave me the strong work ethic I still live by to this day. As I have learned being in the tattoo and piercing industry, there is so much to learn. The industry changes depending on the trends, but also stays true to its core traditional values. I feel blessed to be apart of this incredible family of artists and piercers that we have here at Untamed.
