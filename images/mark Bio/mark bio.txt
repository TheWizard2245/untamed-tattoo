My name is Mark Brown Owner of Untamed Tattoos & Piercings, I have been tattooing for 35 years and going into my 24th year at my shop. I was born in Woonsocket RI, but my four siblings and I lived around the country due to our dad being in the Air Force. Junior high is when I realized I had a passion for art and drawing which kept me in the art field throughout high-school. In 1993 after graduating high school I found my love for tattooing and started my apprenticeship. I completed my American Art Institute courses in 1994. Then in 2002 , I received my art degree from United Tribes Technical College in Bismarck North Dakota.
In early 2003 I came back to Woonsocket and by that summer I had opened up Untamed in Bellingham, MA. For the first eight years I did the tattoos and piercings on my own which was not easy, but it has been rewarding because my business has grown over the years. I now have multiple talented artists, and piercers.

I specialize in American traditional, black & gray and cover ups. I am a master piercer, and do air brushing. I also do Traditional Native American bead work which I learned from my time on the Crow Creek Sioux Tribe Reservation in South Dakota.
