Hi, I’m Katie! I’m a tattoo apprentice, nursing student, and mom with a lifelong love for art, creativity, and connecting with people. I’ve always been someone who loves having an outlet to create, and tattooing has given me the opportunity to turn that creativity into something meaningful that people get to carry with them. I especially love fine-line tattoos, florals, script, and feminine designs, but as an apprentice, I’m constantly learning, experimenting, and pushing myself to grow into a well-rounded artist. I love working with my clients to take an idea—whether it’s deeply meaningful or just something they think is really freaking cool—and turn it into a piece that feels like them.

Outside of tattooing, I’m a mom to an amazing little girl, a dog mom, and currently working my way through nursing school. My life is usually busy, a little chaotic, and filled with about twelve things happening at once, but I genuinely wouldn’t have it any other way. I care deeply about people and want everyone who sits in my chair to feel comfortable, heard, and completely free to be themselves. Tattooing is still the beginning of a much bigger journey for me, and I’m incredibly excited to keep learning, growing, and creating art I’m proud to put my name behind.
